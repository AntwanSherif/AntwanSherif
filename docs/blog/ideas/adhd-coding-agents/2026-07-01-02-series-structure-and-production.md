# Series structure & production model

**Decision:** Ship the material as a **3-part series**, written as one connected unit and published spaced-out.

**Structure (publish order):**
1. **ADHD in engineering** — personal-led (not an explainer), seeds the agents thread on page one so readers stick for the moat.
2. **ADHD × coding agents** — THE MOAT. The double-edged-sword reframe: parallelism amplifies both the superpower and the overwhelm. Grounded in mechanisms + Gloria Mark interruption science + community proof-of-pain.
3. **The playbook** — tactics as *applied ADHD interventions* (rename sessions = externalize working memory; visual plans = reduce text-parse load; the agent = digital body double / external executive function).

**Talk:** maps to Post 2 as its spine, drawing the human open from Post 1 and the tactics from Post 3. The series *is* the talk, unrolled — they don't compete.

**Production model:** Prepare all 3 together for full narrative control (plant setups in Post 1 that pay off in Post 3; identical voice throughout), then publish on a spaced schedule. Cadence is a pure scheduling decision, decoupled from writing.

**Writing order (internal):** anchor on Post 2 (the moat / freshest thinking) to de-risk the thesis, then Post 1 on-ramp, then Post 3 payoff — but deliver all three as a coherent set.

**Cross-referencing:** posts cross-link each other AND the author's other posts/work. This is an instance of a **blog-wide standing rule** (see memory: blog-posts-cross-reference-rule) — to be codified in the blog's intent-layer.

**Shared glossary:** ADHD terminology is built once via `/teach` as **structured data**, consumed by two surfaces: (a) the teach learning-reference, and (b) an MDX `<Term>` component in the posts rendering a dashed underline + hover/click definition. One source of truth; the cheat-sheet and the posts can never drift. Component is a build task pending glossary content.

**Method note:** Research-first (already executed) — see `research-brief.md` and `talks-and-community.md`. Scope was chosen by the material, not pre-committed.

**Trade-off accepted:** Slower to first publish (all 3 written before any ship) in exchange for a fully controlled narrative arc and airtight cross-references.
