# ADHD × Coding Agents — Resources

Curated from the research pass. Full sourcing table + verification tags in `../research-brief.md`.

## Knowledge

- [Song et al. 2021 — Global prevalence of adult ADHD (J Global Health)](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7916320/) ✅ verified
  Peer-reviewed meta-analysis. Use for: the only hard prevalence numbers (2.58% persistent / 6.76% symptomatic, 2020).
- [Alderson et al. — Working-memory deficits in adults with ADHD (meta-analysis, 38 studies)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23688211/) ✅ verified
  Use for: the claim that working-memory deficits are real and persist into adulthood. Spine under "externalize your state" tactics.
- [Time perception in ADHD (meta-analysis, 27 studies)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33302769/) ✅ verified
  Use for: time-blindness. ⚠️ child/adolescent sample skew — check before generalizing to adults.
- [Mark, González & Harris 2005 — "No Task Left Behind?" (CHI'05)](https://ics.uci.edu/~gmark/CHI2005.pdf) primary
  Use for: real interruption numbers (~11 min per working sphere; ~25min26s to resume). NOT the pop "23:15" figure.
- [Mark, Gudith & Klocke 2008 — "The Cost of Interrupted Work" (CHI'08)](https://ics.uci.edu/~gmark/chi08-mark.pdf) primary
  Use for: the killer double-edged fact — interrupted work finishes FASTER but at higher stress/frustration (p<.01); 20 min is enough to spike it.
- [Oberien — "23 minutes 15 seconds" debunk](https://blog.oberien.de/2023/11/05/23-minutes-15-seconds.html) blog / investigative
  Use for: NOT citing the folklore figure. Read before you put any refocus number on a slide.
- [arXiv 2509.12153 — Body doubling for ADHD in VR](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.12153v1) primary
  Use for: strongest body-doubling evidence (AI ≈ human body double, p=1.000) — ⚠️ but n=12.
- [arXiv 2507.06864 — Digital body doubling / co-presence AI](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.06864) primary
  Use for: names ADHD mechanisms for knowledge work + bridges body-doubling to AI agents.
- [Cleveland Clinic — Body doubling for ADHD](https://health.clevelandclinic.org/body-doubling-for-adhd) secondary
  Use for: the "external executive functioning" / "assistant following you around" framing.
- [Stack Overflow — What developers with ADHD want you to know](https://stackoverflow.blog/2023/06/05/what-developers-with-adhd-want-you-to-know/) secondary
  Use for: hyperfocus + executive-dysfunction-needs-external-structure, in a dev context.

## Gaps
- No epidemiological study of ADHD prevalence *among software engineers specifically* — the "overrepresented" claim is practitioner opinion only.
- "Interest-based nervous system" (Dodson) is a widely-cited practitioner framework, **not** peer-reviewed — needs honest framing, not a science citation.
- HackerNoon "same failure modes" essay (a great framing device) currently 403s — unverified.
